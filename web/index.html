A NOTE ON THE METHOD

Numbers offer perspective.
You make the edit.

What we measure

The Coleman–Liau index estimates English reading grade level from letters, words, and sentences. A lower number can suggest simpler text. It doesn’t tell you whether your writing is thoughtful, accurate, or worth reading.

What to keep in mind

Sentence detection uses punctuation. Abbreviations and decimals can throw it off, and non-English text isn’t supported. Highlights flag length, not mistakes. Your text is processed locally and isn’t saved after a refresh.
